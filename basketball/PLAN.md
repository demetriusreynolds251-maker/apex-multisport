# Plan: Apex for basketball, done the tennis way (10/31/2026)
Full plan page: https://claude.ai/artifact/MLpjnGUUEMs7GjXAHJbHzD

## The short version
"Pick the best ones" is not a plan. Tennis works because we built our own guess of what each player will do, found facts known before the match that separate winners from losers, and proved each on old results before it touched a board. This plan does the same for NBA, one step at a time, with a stop sign after every step. Nothing goes live without his go. Why basketball first: season runs to June, PrizePicks posts the most picks there, ~20,700 basketball games already downloaded from SportsGameOdds.

## The tennis recipe
1. Save everything (every PP prop + result in one database).
2. Our own guess first (Win Probability from player stats; books are a check).
3. Find the facts that matter (keep only facts that clearly split hits from misses, e.g. 3+ games room 79% vs under 3 54%).
4. Hold what doesn't work (losing prop types to backups).
5. Rank and replay (Win Prob + points per good fact; replay days in slip terms).
6. Prove it on days we didn't build on.
7. Paper test, then live.

Lesson: "books disagree with PrizePicks" alone was a coin flip (Feb-Oct 2026); the edge came from our own guess plus facts. Basketball needs its own guess.

## Coaches and teammates, turned into testable facts
Minutes (steady vs moved around); Blowout risk (big spread, starters sit in 4th); Played yesterday (back-to-back); Teammate out (more shots); Opponent (allows a lot to a position); Room (PP line vs book expectation). If no fact can protect a pick, we stop.

## Steps (stop sign after each)
- Step 1: Check the download (read only): games with PP line + book lines + final player stats together; ESPN box scores if stats missing.
- Step 2: Build player history: one row per player per game (minutes, pts, reb, ast, threes, etc. + rest days + spread).
- Step 3: Basketball Win Probability from recent games, minutes, opponent, rest; check it's honest (70% calls hit ~7 of 10).
- Step 4: Test the facts one at a time then together; keep only ones that help on both halves of the season.
- Step 5: Replay Top 3 and Elite 5 in slip terms; build on last season's first half, check on second half + this season; report days won and dollars at $[amount].
- Step 6: Paper test 2-3 weeks in a new test channel with its own tracker, TEST FINISHED card at end.
- Step 7: Live only on his go; cards, slips, #pnl, tax ledger carry over with a sport column.
